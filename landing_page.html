how do we detect ...

IRIS uses MediaPipe and OpenCV with a ResNeXt CNN and an RNN to accurately detect blending boundaries, lighting mismatches and texture inconsistencies.

  • Facial Bounding & Alignment

    Employs MediaPipe and OpenCV to isolate and align face crops (e.g., 224 × 224 pixels) while discarding background regions to eliminate background shortcut learning.

  • Spatial Feature Extraction

    Utilizes a ResNeXt CNN (with parallel cardinality paths) or a lightweight backbone like MobileNetV2 / EfficientNet to analyze per-frame pixel artifacts.

  • Temporal Modeling

    Utilizes a Recurrent Neural Network (LSTM or GRU) to capture inter-frame motion, exposing temporal jitter, flickering, and unnatural lip/eye dynamics across sequential frames.

  • Temporal Confidence Graphing

    Generates per-frame confidence scores to visualize exactly where and when inconsistencies occur across the timeline.
